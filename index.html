Inle Bush

I'm a PhD student in the Program in Neuroscience at Harvard, supported by the NSF GRFP. I received my B.A. in Computer Science, Biology, & Mathematics from Cornell University. Previously, I worked in the lab of Chun Han, studying dendritic development and structural plasticity, the lab of Saket Navlakha, modelling plant-root and NMJ development, and the lab of Vanessa Ruta, studying the evolution of territorial aggression.
