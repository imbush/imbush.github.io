I study neuroscience as a PhD student at Harvard Medical School. I am interested in the development and evolution of neural circuits. 

I did my undergrad at Cornell University, where I studied Computer Science, Biology & Mathematics. I have worked in the lab of [Chun Han](https://han.wicmb.cornell.edu/), studying dendritic development and structural plasticity, the lab of [Saket Navlakha](https://navlakhalab.net/), studying plant-root and NMJ development, and the lab of [Vanessa Ruta](https://www.therutalab.com/) advised by [Philipp Brand](https://evolvingors.wordpress.com/), studying behavioral evolution in _Drosophila_. 

I am Burmese-American and grew up in New Hampshire. In my free-time I enjoy [reading](https://www.goodreads.com/user/show/135165176-inle-bush), biking, and climbing with friends. On this site, you'll find highlights of my [nature photography]({{ '/photography/' | relative_url }}) from the US Northeast.
