I'm a senior (applying to doctoral programs!) at Cornell University working towards degrees in Computer Science, Biology, & Mathematics. Through my research, I study cellular mechanisms and computational models of morphogenesis. I work in the lab of [Chun Han](https://han.wicmb.cornell.edu/), studying dendritic development and structural plasticity, and the lab of [Saket Navlakha](https://navlakhalab.net/), modelling plant-root and NMJ development. I am Burmese-american, grew up in New Hampshire, and enjoy [reading](https://www.goodreads.com/user/show/135165176-inle-bush), biking, and climbing with friends.
