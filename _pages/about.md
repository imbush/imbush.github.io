I'm a senior (applying to doctoral programs!) at Cornell University working towards degrees in Biology, Computer Science, & Mathematics. I research nervous system development and evolution. I work in the lab of [Chun Han](https://han.wicmb.cornell.edu/), studying dendritic development and structural plasticity. Previously, I studied plant-root and NMJ development with [Saket Navlakha](https://navlakhalab.net/). I worked in the lab of [Vanessa Ruta](https://www.therutalab.com/) advised by [Philipp Brand](https://evolvingors.wordpress.com/), studying behavioral evolution in _Drosophila_. I am Burmese-american, grew up in New Hampshire, and enjoy [reading](https://www.goodreads.com/user/show/135165176-inle-bush), biking, and climbing with friends.
